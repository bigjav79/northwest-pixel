Meet the founder

Local experience. A bigger vision.

With years of experience in telecommunications and technology sales, Javi Gutierrez founded Northwest Pixel to help small businesses get the same high-quality digital tools as large companies, without the high costs or complexity.

  • Based in the PNW
  • Serving Idaho, Oregon & Washington
  • Small business supporter
  • Real people, real support

At Northwest Pixel, we believe every small business deserves a professional online presence without the overwhelming costs, complicated processes, or technical headaches.

Founded by Javi Gutierrez, Northwest Pixel is built on years of experience in telecommunications, technology sales, business development, and customer relationships.

Throughout his career, Javi has worked alongside business owners, helping them navigate technology, improve operations, and find new opportunities for growth.

Along the way, he recognized a common problem: too many small businesses were being left behind in an increasingly digital world.

Not because they lacked ambition or great products, but because building a professional website often felt too expensive, too complicated, or simply out of reach.

We created Northwest Pixel to change that.

Built in the Northwest. Built for Your Business.

We're proud to be based in the Pacific Northwest and passionate about helping businesses across Idaho, Oregon, and Washington thrive.

We understand that running a small business means wearing multiple hats. Your time is valuable, your budget matters, and every investment needs to make sense.

That's why we focus on delivering professional, modern, and affordable websites designed with one purpose in mind: helping your business attract more customers and generate more revenue.

More Than Just a Website

A great website should do more than look impressive.

It should build credibility, establish trust, generate leads, and turn visitors into paying customers.

We combine real business experience with practical digital solutions to help small businesses create an online presence that works as hard as they do.

  • No unnecessary complexity.
  • No confusing technical jargon.
  • No overpriced solutions.

Just straightforward service, transparent communication, and websites built with your business goals in mind.
